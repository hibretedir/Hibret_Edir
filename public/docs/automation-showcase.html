Case study · Mutual-assistance platform

End-to-end membership onboarding automation

A 200-member Edir organization replaced spreadsheets and manual tracking with a single pipeline — waiting list through paid membership — with full audit logging at every step.

What the platform automates

Fair queue & cap

Ordered waiting list with live slot math against a 200-member cap — no over-inviting.

Vet before billing

Board reviews name, application, and ID before any PayPal invoice is sent.

Pay to activate

$200 registration invoice via PayPal; active member record created only when paid.

Full audit trail

Every invite, submission, review, invoice, and approval logged for board governance.

Automation by trigger

1 · Public waiting list signup
Applicant submits name, email, phone on the public site.
  • Validates and stores queue position
  • Updates public waiting list status
  • Writes audit log entry
2 · Board sends invitation
Board clicks Send Invitation when a membership slot is open.
  • Verifies slot availability (cap − active − in pipeline)
  • Emails application link via SendGrid
  • Status → Invited to Apply; holds slot
3 · Application submitted
Invited applicant completes form + ID upload at /application/.
  • Creates membership_applications record
  • Notifies board (email/SMS when configured)
  • Appears in Applications → Pending
4 · Board approves & sends invoice
After name, fields, and ID checks — Approve & Send Invoice.
  • Creates & sends $200 PayPal registration invoice
  • Status → Awaiting Payment
  • Applicant notified with payment link
5 · Payment received
PayPal sync (9 AM & 6 PM Pacific) or board Mark Registration Paid.
  • Creates Active member + beneficiary in CRM
  • Records $200 payment; links application
  • Waiting list → Added as Member; welcome notification
