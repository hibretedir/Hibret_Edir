How It Works
እንዴት እንደሚሰራ

Mutual Support When It Matters Most

በጣም አስፈላጊ ጊዜ የጋራ ድጋፍ

When a member or their immediate family passes away, our community comes together to provide financial and emotional support for the grieving family.

አባል ወይም ቤተሰቡ ሲሞት፣ ማህበረሰባችን አብሮ ለሚያዝነው ቤተሰብ ድጋፍ ለማድረግ ይሰበሰባል።

→
1

Member Passes Away

አባል ይሞታል

When a member or their immediate family (spouse or children up to age 26) passes, the board is notified immediately.

አባል ወይም ቤተሰቡ ሲሞት፣ ቦርዱ ወዲያውኑ ይነገረዋል።

→
2

All Members Notified

ሁሉም አባላት ይነገሯቸዋል

An announcement is sent to all 195+ members via SMS, email, and WhatsApp with payment instructions and funeral details.

ማስታወቂያ ለሁሉም አባላት SMS፣ ኢሜይል እና WhatsApp በኩል ይላካል።

→
3

Members Contribute $110

አባላት $110 ይከፍላሉ

Each active member contributes $110 within 3 days via PayPal, Zelle, or direct deposit to the BofA account.

እያንዳንዱ ንቁ አባል $110 በ3 ቀናት ውስጥ ይከፍላል።

4

Family Receives $15,000

ቤተሰብ $15,000 ይቀበላል

The grieving family receives $15,000 to cover funeral and burial costs, bringing dignity and relief during a painful time.

ቤተሰቡ $15,000 ለቀብር ወጪ ይቀበላል።

About Us
ስለ እኛ

A Community Built on Unity

በአንድነት የተገነባ ማህበረሰብ

Founded in 2011 by Ethiopian families in the Greater Los Angeles area, Hibret Edir was created to bring the tradition of Edir — a deeply rooted Ethiopian mutual aid practice — to our diaspora community.

እ.ኤ.አ. በ2011 በሎስ አንጀለስ ኢትዮጵያዊ ቤተሰቦች የተመሰረተው ሃብረት እድር፣ የኢትዮጵያን የእድር ወግ ለስደተኛ ማህበረሰባችን ለማምጣት ተቋቁሟል።

We operate with full transparency — using modern technology to manage contributions, track payments, and ensure every family receives support quickly and with dignity. Our mission is simple: no Ethiopian family in Los Angeles should face the burden of funeral costs alone.

ዓላማችን ቀላል ነው፡ በሎስ አንጀለስ ምንም ኢትዮጵያዊ ቤተሰብ 혼자 የቀብር ወጪን መሸከም የለበትም።

Our Rules & Governance

ደንቦቻችን እና አስተዳደር

Hibret Edir operates under a transparent by-law adopted by members in 2011 and updated in 2015. Key highlights below.

Article 2

Membership Eligibility

የአባልነት ብቃት

Any person of Ethiopian origin within 50 miles of Downtown Los Angeles. All members have equal rights to vote and participate.

ከዳውንታውን ሎስ አንጀለስ 50 ማይል ርቀት ውስጥ ያሉ ኢትዮጵያዊ ተወላጆች።

Article 6

Financial Contributions

የገንዘብ አስተዋፅኦ

$200 initial membership fee. $110 per event contribution due within 3 days of notification. Members may maintain a credit balance.

$200 የመጀመሪያ አባልነት ክፍያ። $110 በ3 ቀናት ውስጥ።

Article 7

Edir Payout

የእድር ክፍያ

$15,000 paid to the member's family or designated beneficiary. Payment can be made urgently from the emergency fund if needed.

$15,000 ለቤተሰቡ ወይም ለተወካዩ ይከፈላል።

Article 8

Family Coverage

የቤተሰብ ሽፋን

Coverage includes the member, their spouse, and biological or legally adopted children up to age 26.

ሽፋን አባሉን፣ ትዳር አጋሩን እና እስከ 26 ዓመት ያሉ ልጆችን ያካትታል።

Article 10

New Member Benefits

የአዲስ አባል ጥቅሞች

New members must be active for 4 months before drawing financial benefits. Accidental death benefits are immediate.

አዲስ አባላት ከ4 ወር በኋላ የገንዘብ ጥቅም ያገኛሉ።

Article 11

Payment Requirements

የክፍያ መስፈርቶች

Late payment after 3 days. Full payment required within 45 days or membership may be terminated after 4 consecutive delinquencies.

45 ቀናት ውስጥ ሙሉ ክፍያ። 4 ተከታታይ ዘገያ ካለ አባልነት ሊቋረጥ ይችላል።
