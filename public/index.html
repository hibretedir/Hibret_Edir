How It Works
እንዴት ይሰራል

Mutual Support When It Matters Most

በጣም አስፈላጊ ጊዜ የጋራ ድጋፍ

When a member passes, our entire community comes together to support the grieving family financially and emotionally.

አባል ሲሞት፣ ማህበረሰባችን ሙሉ ድጋፍ ለቤተሰቡ ይሰጣል።

1

Member Passes Away

አባል ይሞታል

The board is notified and immediately begins the support process for the grieving family.

ቦርዱ ወዲያውኑ ይነገረዋል።

2

All Members Notified

ሁሉም አባላት ይነገሯቸዋል

SMS, email, and WhatsApp sent to all — active members with payment instructions and funeral details.

SMS፣ ኢሜይል እና WhatsApp ለሁሉም አባላት ይላካል።

3

Members Contribute $110

አባላት $110 ይከፍላሉ

Each active member contributes $110 per death within 3 days via PayPal, Zelle, or direct deposit.

እያንዳንዱ አባል በሞት $110 በ3 ቀናት ውስጥ ይከፍላል።

4

Family Receives $15,000

ቤተሰብ $15,000 ይቀበላል

The family receives $15,000, minus service charge, to cover funeral and burial costs with dignity and speed.

ቤተሰቡ $15,000 (ከአገልግሎት ክፍያ በስተቀር) ለቀብር ወጪ በክብር እና በፍጥነት ይቀበላል።

About Us
ስለ እኛ

A Community Built on Unity

በአንድነት የተገነባ ማህበረሰብ

Founded in 2011 by Ethiopian families in Greater Los Angeles, Hibret Edir brings the ancient tradition of mutual aid to our diaspora community. No Ethiopian family should face the burden of funeral costs alone.

እ.ኤ.አ. በ2011 የተቋቋመው ሕብረት እድር ለኢትዮጵያዊ ቤተሰቦች ድጋፍ ይሰጣል።

By-Laws
ደንብ

Our Rules & Governance

ደንቦቻችን

Operating under a transparent by-law adopted in 2011. Key highlights below.

እ.ኤ.አ. 2011 የተቀበለው ግልጽ ደንብ። ዋና ነጥቦች ከዚህ በታች።

Article 2አንቀጽ 2

Membership Eligibility

የአባልነት ብቃት

Any Ethiopian origin within 50 miles of Downtown LA. All members have equal rights.

ከ50 ማይል ርቀት ውስጥ ያሉ ኢትዮጵያዊ ተወላጆች።

Article 6አንቀጽ 6

Financial Contributions

የገንዘብ አስተዋፅኦ

$200 initial fee. $110 per death due within 3 days.

$200 የመጀመሪያ ክፍያ። በሞት $110 በ3 ቀናት ውስጥ።

Article 7አንቀጽ 7

Edir Payout

የእድር ክፍያ

$15,000 paid to member's designated beneficiary.

$15,000 ለአባሉ የተሰየመ ተቀባይ ይከፈላል።

Article 8አንቀጽ 8

Family Coverage

የቤተሰብ ሽፋን

Covers the member, spouse, and biological or legally adopted children up to age 26.

ሽፋን አባሉን፣ ትዳር አጋሩን እና እስከ 26 ዓመት ያሉ ልጆችን ያካትታል።

Article 10አንቀጽ 10

New Member Benefits

የአዲስ አባል ጥቅሞች

Must be active 4 months before drawing benefits. Accidental death benefits are immediate.

አዲስ አባላት ከ4 ወር በኋላ ጥቅም ያገኛሉ።

Article 11አንቀጽ 11

Payment Requirements

የክፍያ መስፈርቶች

Full payment within 45 days. After 4 consecutive delinquencies, membership may be terminated.

45 ቀናት ውስጥ ሙሉ ክፍያ። 4 ተከታታይ ዘገያ ካለ አባልነት ሊቋረጥ ይችላል።
